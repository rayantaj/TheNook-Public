Welcome to TheNook Support

📖 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my journal private?
A: Absolutely! All your data is stored locally on your device. We don't have servers, we don't collect data, and we never see your journal entries. You can also enable passcode lock with Face ID for extra security.
Q: How do I backup my journal?
A: Go to Settings → Data & Backup → Export Backup. This creates a folder with all your entries and media that you can save to iCloud Drive, Files, or share via AirDrop. To restore, use the Import Backup button.
Q: Can I add photos and videos to my entries?
A: Yes! When creating an entry, tap the camera icon to take a photo, the image icon to select from your library, or the microphone to record audio. You can add multiple photos and videos to each entry.
Q: How do I set up passcode lock?
A: Go to Settings → Privacy → Enable Lock. Follow the prompts to create a 6-digit passcode. If your device supports Face ID or Touch ID, the app will use biometric authentication.
Q: What happens if I delete the app?
A: All your journal entries will be deleted from your device. Make sure to export a backup first if you want to keep your entries! Go to Settings → Data & Backup → Export Backup before deleting.
Q: Can I use TheNook on multiple devices?
A: Currently, TheNook stores data locally on each device. You can export from one device and import on another to transfer your journal. Automatic sync across devices is planned for a future update.
Q: Does TheNook cost anything?
A: TheNook is completely free with no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads. We believe everyone deserves access to mental wellness tools.
Q: What is the Crisis Support feature?
A: TheNook includes quick access to mental health crisis resources, including national hotlines. If you're in crisis, tap the Crisis Support button in Settings to find immediate help.
Q: Can I change the app's appearance?
A: Yes! Go to Settings to enable dark mode, adjust text size, and customize your name and emoji. More themes coming in future updates.
Q: How do I delete an entry?
A: Swipe left on any entry in your journal list and tap Delete. Or, open the entry and tap the trash icon. Deleted entries cannot be recovered unless you have a backup.

🐛 Troubleshooting

App won't open or crashes
1. Force close the app and reopen
2. Restart your device
3. Ensure you're running iOS 17.0 or later
4. If the problem persists, contact support
Face ID not working
1. Check that Face ID is enabled in iOS Settings
2. Make sure TheNook has permission to use Face ID
3. You can always use the passcode fallback option
Photos not attaching
1. Go to iOS Settings → TheNook → Photos
2. Ensure "Selected Photos" or "All Photos" is enabled
3. Grant camera permission if taking new photos
Backup export failed
1. Ensure you have enough storage space
2. Try exporting to a different location
3. Close other apps to free up memory
